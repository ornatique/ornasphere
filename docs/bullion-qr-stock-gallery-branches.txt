Bullion, QR Making and Stock Gallery: branch selection

Implemented locally; not deployed.
Uses existing CompanyBranches and BranchReadOnly services from the earlier branch rollout.
No new routes or database migrations.

WEB
Searchable multiple company/branch selection is available to the main-company admin.
QR Making and Stock Gallery combine selected branches and label each record with its owner.
Bullion lists people from selected branches; selecting a person displays only that person's ledger and balance.
Other branches are read-only. Edit/delete and gallery image changes are disabled; server-side checks reject foreign writes.
QR and Bullion PDFs show the owning company. Gallery PDFs show selected branches and each item's branch.

API
Send either company_id for one company OR management_branches for multiple companies, never both.
Omitting both preserves the logged-in company's scope. Only authorized active branches may be selected.
Examples (replace example IDs with actual company IDs):

GET /api/qr-making?management_branches[]=1&management_branches[]=2
GET /api/qr-making/10/pdf?company_id=2
GET /api/stock-gallery?management_branches[]=1&management_branches[]=2&per_page=24
GET /api/stock-gallery/pdf?management_branches[]=1&management_branches[]=2&direct=1
GET /api/bullion/persons?management_branches[]=1&management_branches[]=2
GET /api/bullion/balance?company_id=2&person_id=191
GET /api/bullion/entries?company_id=2&person_id=191
GET /api/bullion/pdf?company_id=2&person_id=191

Stock Gallery PDF also accepts POST JSON:
{"management_branches":[1,2],"scope":"filtered","direct":1}

List records expose company_id, company_name and can_manage. Preserve existing pagination response shapes.
Use company_id from the chosen record for QR detail/download requests.
Bullion balances/entries/PDF require person_id and remain per person, even when multiple branches are selected.
POST /api/bullion/entries and QR create/update/delete remain restricted to the logged-in company.
